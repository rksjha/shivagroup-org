Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shiva Consultancy Group and its group entities, including Shubham Agribusiness & Financial Solution ("SCG", "we", "us"), collect, use, store and share information through this website (shivagroup.org.in), our YouTube channel (@shivacsg), and our internal publishing tool "SCG Publisher" (the "API Client").

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The SCG Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on SCG's own YouTube channel. By using any SCG service that relies on YouTube API Services, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube API Services are provided by Google, and Google's use of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Through the API Client: the API Client is used only by authorised SCG staff. With OAuth consent from SCG's own Google account, it accesses that channel's basic details (channel ID, title), our own video and playlist metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy and schedule status) and the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to perform uploads. It does not access data of any other YouTube user.
  • Through this website: information you choose to send us (name, email, phone, organisation, message) via contact or booking links, and standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) recorded by our hosting provider and, where used, analytics cookies.

3. How We Use Information

  • To publish, schedule and organise SCG's own videos on YouTube and to verify that uploads succeeded.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide our advisory services.
  • To maintain the security and performance of our website and tools.

We do not sell personal information, do not use YouTube API data for advertising, and do not combine it with data from other sources to profile users.

4. Sharing of Information

YouTube API data is shared only with Google/YouTube as needed to perform the requested actions. Website enquiries may be shared with SCG group entities and professional associates solely to service your request, or where required by law. We do not share information with other third parties.

5. Storage, Retention and Security

OAuth tokens and video metadata are stored in access-controlled Google Cloud / Google Workspace services used by SCG. Metadata is kept only as long as needed to manage the relevant video, and authorised API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Enquiry data is retained for as long as needed for the engagement and our legal obligations.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies stored on or read from your device for basic functionality and anonymous usage statistics. The API Client does not place cookies on the devices of members of the public. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Choices and Revoking Access

Any Google account that has authorised an SCG application can revoke that access at any time via the Google security settings page. You may also ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold by contacting us below. We process personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
